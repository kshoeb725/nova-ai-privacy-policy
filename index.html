Lawful Grounds for Processing Personal Data

In accordance with Sections 4 and 6 of the DPDP Act 2023, personal data is processed solely under the following lawful bases:

  • Explicit Affirmative Consent (Section 6): Optional third-party cloud intelligence (Google Gemini API) is disabled by default and activated only upon your explicit, informed, affirmative consent within the Application Settings.
  • User-Directed Execution: On-device utilities (such as alarms, flashlight toggling, contact calling, and local media playback) execute solely in direct response to your specific, real-time commands.
  • Scope of Cloud AI Consent Withdrawal: You retain the right to withdraw Cloud AI consent at any time in Settings. Withdrawing Cloud AI consent immediately halts outbound requests and aborts in-flight network connections to the Google Gemini API. However, withdrawing Cloud AI consent does not affect separate, user-initiated safety/bug reporting (which is an independent, opt-in feature triggered only if you tap 'Report Issue'), nor does it override or disable the Android operating system's platform SpeechRecognizer or TextToSpeech providers, which operate under system settings.

On-Device Processing & System Provider Realities

Nova is built on an on-device-first foundation. The precise technical reality of data handling for each feature is detailed below:

  • Voice Input & Speech Recognition (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Nova interfaces with the Android platform's native SpeechRecognizer API. Microphone audio is processed in volatile memory to convert spoken commands into text. Nova itself does not record, save, or transmit raw audio files to developer servers. However, depending on your device settings and chosen speech recognition provider (such as Google Speech Services or OEM system recognizers), your device's operating system speech provider may perform network processing in accordance with its own privacy policy.
  • Speech Output & Text-to-Speech (TTS): Spoken companion responses are synthesized locally using the device's selected Android TextToSpeech engine. Audio synthesis runs on your device; however, some system TTS engines may download or update voice data files from their respective providers over the network per operating system settings.
  • Contacts & Phone Calling (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS, android.permission.CALL_PHONE): Direct access to your device address book is strictly local and restricted to phonetic name matching when you ask to call or message a contact. The contact database itself is never uploaded or synced to Google Gemini, Supabase, or external servers. However, if you voluntarily speak, type, or include a contact's name or phone number in an AI conversational prompt, or if a contact is displayed on-screen during an 'Explain Screen' capture, that user-provided text or image is transmitted to Gemini as part of the query payload.
  • Personal Memories & Notes (wallpaper_agent_memory.db): Facts, reminders, and preferences you ask Nova or Luma to remember are stored in a local SQLite Room database within the application's private internal storage sandbox. This database is protected by Android OS application sandboxing (Linux UID isolation) and is explicitly excluded from cloud backups. No database-level SQLCipher encryption is applied to the SQLite file itself.
  • Recent Conversation History: Contextual conversation turns (up to 20 turns) are held transiently in volatile RAM during your active session to maintain conversational continuity. This data is never written to disk and is completely cleared when the session is reset or the app process is terminated.
  • Document & File Access (Storage Access Framework - SAF): File search and indexing operate strictly within user-authorized directories granted via Android's system document picker. File contents and index caches remain 100% on-device.
  • Device Settings & System Controls: Toggles for flashlight, brightness, volume, Do Not Disturb, and alarms execute directly through standard Android system APIs without telemetry collection.

Optional Cloud AI Processing (Google Gemini Developer API — Free vs. Paid Tiers)

When you explicitly enable Optional Cloud AI Mode in Settings, conversational queries are processed using Google LLC's Gemini Developer API (via the standard developer REST endpoint generativelanguage.googleapis.com) over HTTPS:

  • Data Transmitted to Gemini: Your typed or transcribed conversational prompt, recent active in-memory conversation context (up to 20 turns), and memory context summaries.
  • Screen Understanding ("Explain Screen"): Only invoked when you explicitly initiate screen explanation and approve Android's system MediaProjection consent prompt. A single static frame is captured into volatile memory, scaled down (max 1024px), and sent to Gemini Vision over HTTPS. Screenshots are never saved to permanent storage.
  • Personal Information in User Content: Conversational prompts and screen captures may themselves contain personal information if you voluntarily type, speak, or display sensitive details. You are advised not to submit confidential or sensitive personal data.
  • Developer API Tiers & Data Use (Free vs. Paid Keys): Under Google's Gemini Developer API terms, when using free-tier quotas or default developer endpoints, Google may log and review prompts and generated content to train and improve Google models and products. If you supply your own paid Google AI Studio or Google Cloud Vertex AI API key in Settings, Google's paid customer terms apply (where prompts are not used to train Google foundation models). In all cases, Nova operates via Google's developer API rather than custom enterprise zero-retention contracts.
  • Cross-Border Transfer (Section 16 DPDP Act): Cloud AI requests are processed in Google Cloud data centers located in the United States and globally in compliance with applicable cross-border transfer rules.

AI Safety Reporting & Third-Party Processors

Nova includes an optional safety reporting feature allowing users to report offensive, harmful, inaccurate, or privacy-concerning AI responses directly to the developer:

  • Voluntary, User-Initiated Submissions: A safety report is never transmitted automatically; it is generated solely when you tap "Report Issue" on an AI response and confirm submission.
  • Opt-In Response Excerpt (Excluded by Default): The AI response excerpt is excluded by default (transmitted as an empty string) unless you explicitly check the opt-in box to include it. Original conversation history and user prompts are never attached.
  • Client-Side Privacy Sanitization: Before transmission, client-side regex sanitizers scan the optional excerpt and feedback to redact email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, and cryptographic keys. Any excerpt containing multiple sensitive entities or private keys is automatically dropped entirely.
  • Serverless Relay (Supabase Edge Functions) & Email Delivery (Resend): Reports are transmitted over HTTPS to a Supabase Edge Function (submit-ai-report) that verifies rate limits and forwards the report via Resend's transactional email service directly to the developer's mailbox.
  • Distinction from Rate Limiting: No persistent database table of report contents or user profiles is stored in Supabase. Supabase maintains only an ephemeral rate-limiting table storing salted, cryptographic SHA-256 hashes of client identifiers with an automatic 10-minute expiration. Hashed identifiers are pseudonymous, and expiration is distinct from instantaneous physical erasure of cloud platform infrastructure logs.
  • Email Retention in Developer Mailbox: Reports delivered to the developer's mailbox are retained as email correspondence for bug remediation, quality assurance, and AI safety analysis until manually archived or deleted.
  • Third-Party Processors: Supabase Inc. (edge function execution and rate limiting) and Resend Inc. (transactional email dispatch) process report data and standard connection metadata (IP addresses, timestamps) in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

User Privacy Rights, Data Erasure & Uninstall Clarification

Under Section 12 of the DPDP Act 2023 and Google Play User Data Policies, you have complete control over your personal data:

  • Delete Individual Memories: Tap the delete icon next to any saved fact in Settings → Memory & Privacy to immediately erase it from local SQLite storage.
  • Clear All Memories: Tap "Clear All Memories" in Settings → Memory & Privacy to physically purge all saved notes and facts from local storage.
  • Purge Conversation History: Tap "Clear Conversation History" in Settings → AI & Offline Mode to instantly wipe volatile in-memory context.
  • Withdraw Cloud AI Consent: Toggle off "Cloud AI Mode" in Settings at any time to immediately cease all communication with Google Gemini.
  • App Uninstall vs. Third-Party Data: Uninstalling Nova completely deletes all local databases, preferences, and Keystore keys from your device. However, uninstalling the app does not delete data previously transmitted to third parties (such as report emails already delivered to the developer inbox, or technical connection logs held by Google Gemini, Supabase, or Resend). To request deletion of prior email correspondence, contact the developer at shoebbussiness786@gmail.com.

  • प्रतिक्रिया अंश डिफ़ॉल्ट रूप से बाहर: प्रतिक्रिया का अंश डिफ़ॉल्ट रूप से शामिल नहीं होता (खाली भेजा जाता है) जब तक कि आप टिक न करें। मूल प्रश्न या पूरा इतिहास कभी नहीं भेजा जाता।
  • डिवाइस पर गोपनीयता फ़िल्टर: भेजने से पहले नाम, फोन नंबर, ईमेल, आईपी और क्रिप्टोग्राफ़िक कुंजियों को स्थानीय रूप से हटा दिया जाता है।
  • सर्वरलेस रिले (Supabase) व ईमेल डिलीवरी (Resend): रिपोर्ट एन्क्रिप्टेड HTTPS द्वारा Supabase Edge Function पर जाती है और Resend के माध्यम से सीधे डेवलपर के मेलबॉक्स पर भेजी जाती है।
  • रेट-लिमिटिंग से अंतर: Supabase में रिपोर्ट सामग्री का कोई स्थायी डेटाबेस नहीं होता। केवल दर-सीमा लागू करने के लिए हेक्साडेसिमल हैश १० मिनट के लिए अस्थायी रूप से रखे जाते हैं। हैश छद्मनामी (pseudonymous) होते हैं। क्लाउड सर्वर लॉग तुरंत नष्ट होने की गारंटी नहीं होती।
  • ईमेल प्रतिधारण: डेवलपर मेलबॉक्स में प्राप्त रिपोर्ट ईमेल बग निवारण और सुरक्षा समीक्षा के लिए मैन्युअल रूप से हटाए जाने तक संग्रहीत रहते हैं।
  • तृतीय-पक्ष प्रोसेसर: Supabase Inc. और Resend Inc. अपनी गोपनीयता नीतियों के तहत रिपोर्ट डेटा और तकनीकी कनेक्शन मेटाडेटा प्रोसेस करते हैं।

सुरक्षा उपाय, एन्क्रिप्शन और बैकअप नियम

एंड्रॉइड क्लाउड बैकअप से बाहर: आपकी गोपनीयता के लिए, सभी निजी डेटाबेस (wallpaper_agent_memory.db, ai_response_reports.db), कीस्टोर कुंजियां और SAF टोकन Google Drive क्लाउड बैकअप और डिवाइस ट्रांसफर से पूरी तरह बाहर रखे गए हैं (backup_rules.xml और data_extraction_rules.xml)।

  • सैंडबॉक्स सुरक्षा: स्थानीय SQLite डेटाबेस ऐप के निजी आंतरिक स्टोरेज में एंड्रॉइड लिनक्स UID अलगाव द्वारा सुरक्षित है। डेटाबेस फ़ाइल पर कोई SQLCipher एन्क्रिप्शन नहीं है।
  • हार्डवेयर कीस्टोर: कस्टम जेमिनी एपीआई की डिवाइस के सिक्योर चिप (AndroidKeyStore TEE/StrongBox) में AES-256 GCM से सुरक्षित हैं।
  • कोई रिमोट खाता नहीं: नोवा में पासवर्ड या क्लाउड लॉगिन की आवश्यकता नहीं है, जिससे डेटा उल्लंघन का कोई केंद्रीय खतरा नहीं रहता।
